Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

This policy explains what the Campus Connect app collects, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and the control you have over it. It applies to the Campus Connect apps for iPhone and Android and to this website.

The short version: we collect what the app needs to work. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. You can delete your account at any time.

What we collect

DataExamplesWhy
Account detailsName, university email address, university, password (stored only as a secure hash)To create and secure your account and confirm you are a student
ProfileProfile picture, bio, programme, year of study, courses, interestsTo show your profile and suggest people with shared courses or interests
Content you createPosts, comments, stories, polls, events, study groups and the photos or videos you attachTo show it to the people you shared it with
MessagesDirect messages and the photos or videos you send in themTo deliver them to the people you are talking to

Device informationA push notification token, and your sign-in sessionTo send the notifications you turned on and keep you signed in
ReportsPosts you report and the reason you giveTo review content that may break our Terms

We do not use advertising identifiers, third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Location

  • Location is requested only when you open a feature that needs it, not on first launch.
  • It is collected only while the app is open. Campus Connect does not track your location in the background.
  • In Settings → Privacy you can stop appearing to nearby students, or show only an approximate distance instead of your exact position.
  • You can also turn off location for Campus Connect at any time in your phone's settings.

Who can see your information

  • Other students: your profile and posts are visible according to your privacy settings and the audience you choose for each post (public, your university, or your connections). Stories expire after 24 hours.
  • People you message see the messages you send them.
  • Our team may review content that has been reported, and act on accounts that break our Terms.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the service. They process data only on our behalf and only to provide their service to us:

  • Hosting and databases: Render (application servers), Hostinger (database hosting) and MongoDB Atlas (messages and location data).
  • Media storage: Cloudinary stores and serves the photos and videos you upload.
  • Notifications: Expo, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Email: a transactional email provider sends verification codes and account emails.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal information or share it with anyone for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your information for as long as your account is open.
  • Stories are removed after 24 hours.
  • When you delete your account, it is deactivated immediately and your profile is removed from the app. We keep it for 30 days so you can recover it if you change your mind. After 30 days, your account and the content linked to it are permanently deleted.
  • We may keep limited records for longer if the law requires it, or to deal with a safety report.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correct: edit your profile, interests and courses at any time from your profile or in Settings.
  • Control visibility: use Settings → Privacy, and choose an audience each time you post.
  • Notifications: turn them on or off in Settings → Notifications or in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account: in the app go to Settings → Account → Delete account, or see Delete your account to ask us by email.
  • Get a copy of your data: email us at __SUPPORT_EMAIL__.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) or the GDPR. To use any of them, contact us. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

Passwords are stored as secure hashes, and data is sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

Campus Connect is for university students. It is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy significantly, we will let you know in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
