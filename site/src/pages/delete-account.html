Delete your account

You can delete your Campus Connect account at any time, from the app or by asking us.

In the app

  1. Open the You tab and tap the menu icon to open Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

Without the app

Email __SUPPORT_EMAIL__ with the subject Delete my account, from the email address you signed up with. We'll confirm, then delete your account within 7 days.

What happens to your data

  • Your account is deactivated immediately. You're signed out, and your profile no longer appears in the app.
  • For 30 days you can change your mind. Email us and we'll restore your account.
  • After 30 days, your account, profile, posts, comments, stories, messages, connections and location data are permanently deleted.
  • We may keep limited records for longer if the law requires it, or to deal with a safety report. See our Privacy Policy.
