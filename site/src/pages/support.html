Account and sign-in

I didn't get my verification code

Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you entered your university email address correctly. You can ask for a new code on the verification screen. If it still doesn't arrive, email us from that address.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password?. We'll email you a code to set a new password.

How do I change my profile?

Open the You tab and tap Edit profile, or go to Settings → Account.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm with your password. You can also ask us to delete it.

Safety and privacy

How do I report a post?

Tap ••• on the post, then Report post, and choose a reason. Our team reviews reports, normally within 24 hours. To report a person or a message, email us with their name and what happened.

How do I stop seeing a post?

Tap ••• on the post, then Hide this post. To see hidden posts again, go to Settings → Hidden posts.

Who can see my location?

Only students using the nearby feature, and only if you allow it. Location is used only while the app is open. In Settings → Privacy you can stop appearing to nearby students, or show only an approximate distance. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Go to Settings → Notifications, or change them for Campus Connect in your phone's settings.

Something isn't working

The app is slow to load at first

The first time you open the app after a while, it can take a few seconds to connect. If it doesn't load after a minute, check your internet connection, then close and reopen the app.

My university isn't listed

Email us with your university's name and we'll look into adding it.
